# Release notes

Every release gets notes written for clinic staff, not for developers.

## What goes in

- New things a practitioner or receptionist can do, in one sentence each.
- Changes to something they already use, with a screenshot.
- Fixes they reported, named the way they reported them.

## What stays out

Internal refactors, dependency upgrades and anything behind a flag that is
still off.

## When

Notes are drafted during the freeze, reviewed by support, and published with
the release in the in-app "What's new" panel.
